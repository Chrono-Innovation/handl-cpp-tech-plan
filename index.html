Compensation & payroll system

The AWS architecture behind clinic onboarding and bi-weekly payroll execution.

Why four accounts rather than one. A boundary only exists where a role or a key could not do the same job. cpp-ingest is the one account with a route to the internet and the one place unfiltered EMR data ever lands. cpp-audit receives a trail that production is not permitted to rewrite or delete. cpp-shared keeps the right to change the system separate from the right to operate it. Onboarding deliberately stays inside cpp-prod — what matters there is which role may promote a rule, and that is IAM, not network. The distinction is failure mode: an account boundary with a policy on top fails closed, where in-account role separation fails open the moment someone writes a permissive rule.
